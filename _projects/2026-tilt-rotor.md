  <section class="tr-section">
    <p class="tr-kicker">01 / OVERVIEW</p>
    <h2>Engineering Challenge</h2>
    <p>Our 35-lb hybrid eVTOL aircraft requires a tilt-rotor mechanism that transitions the motors between vertical and horizontal flight. I owned the mechanical development of the system, including dynamic load modeling, actuator selection and validation, structural analysis, manufacturing, assembly, and testing.</p>
    <div class="tr-callout"><strong>Design objective</strong><p>Build a rigid, manufacturable mechanism that supports demanding transition loads while integrating with ArduPilot-based flight controls.</p></div>
  </section>

  <section class="tr-section">
    <p class="tr-kicker">02 / ANALYSIS</p>
    <h2>Dynamics and Load Modeling</h2>
    <p>I modeled the gyroscopic and inertial loads generated by aircraft motion and rotor tilting. Some analyzed operating cases produced gyroscopic moments exceeding 10 N·m, informing the load cases used to size and evaluate the bracket and shaft.</p>
    <div class="tr-grid">
      <figure class="tr-figure"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/tilt-rotor/fbd.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Tilt-rotor free-body diagram with body axes and applied loads" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Free-body diagram and coordinate definitions.</figcaption></figure>
      <figure class="tr-figure"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/tilt-rotor/load-plot.png' | relative_url }}" alt="Calculated moment versus tilt angle" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Calculated load cases from the dynamics model.</figcaption></figure>
    </div>
    <p><strong>Engineering approach:</strong> rigid-body moment balance about the tilt pivot, including angular acceleration, gyroscopic coupling, and acceleration of the reference point. I am developing a MATLAB tool to evaluate revised geometries and vectored-yaw requirements more efficiently.</p>
  </section>

  <section class="tr-section">
    <p class="tr-kicker">03 / VALIDATION</p>
    <h2>CAD and Structural Analysis</h2>
    <p>I translated the calculated loads into mechanical design requirements and evaluated bracket stress using ANSYS Static Structural. The updated bracket, lengthened for the vectored-yaw configuration, has a calculated factor of safety of 3 against yielding under the analyzed load case.</p>
    <figure class="tr-figure"><img src="{{ '/assets/images/tilt-rotor/ansys.png' | relative_url }}" alt="ANSYS equivalent stress result for the tilt-rotor bracket" loading="lazy"><figcaption>ANSYS equivalent-stress results. Include material, constraints, and applied load case in the final caption.</figcaption></figure>
    <p>The servo actuator was assessed against a 3.5 N·m torque requirement. The actuator torque and bracket gyroscopic moment represent different mechanical requirements and should be labeled with their respective axes and load cases.</p>
  </section>

    <p class="tr-kicker">05 / ITERATION</p>
    <h2>Testing and Redesign</h2>
    <p>I developed test procedures to assess transition speed at varying thrust levels. Following flight testing, I designed and manufactured two revised components in under two weeks to improve rigidity and support faster transitions compatible with our flight-control requirements.</p>
